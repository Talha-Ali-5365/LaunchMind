Core features

Built around the real closing-time workflow

Every feature maps to the operational realities in the spec: quick setup, live availability, time-limited offers, and reliable handoff.

Restaurant onboarding and profile setup

Verify location, set hours, and configure pickup or delivery so operators can launch without extra burden.

Unsold food listings with discount and time window

Post surplus items, quantity, closing-time pickup windows, and discount pricing that feels fair and urgent.

Real-time availability and inventory updates

Remaining quantity updates live and sold-out or expired deals hide automatically to prevent disappointment.

Reservation, pickup, and payment flow

Customers reserve, pay in-app, and receive confirmation with pickup instructions and ETA in one flow.

Food safety, cancellation, and no-show rules

Prominent disclaimers, allergen or condition notes, and clear expiration and no-show policies keep the marketplace trustworthy for everyone involved.
