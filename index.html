Features

Everything needed to move surplus food safely and fast.

Each feature maps directly to the marketplace flow: restaurant publishing, customer discovery, instant reservation, safety expectations, and dependable pickup handoff.

Restaurant Listing Flow

Create end-of-day offers by selecting food type, quantity, pickup window, discount, and availability status in a few taps.

Nearby Discovery and Search

Show customers a map and list of nearby discounted food offers with filters for cuisine, price, pickup time, and dietary needs.

Reservation and Checkout

Allow customers to reserve or purchase instantly, lock inventory, and send confirmation with pickup instructions.

Trust and Safety Controls

Require freshness notes, allergen disclosure, pickup cutoff times, cancellation rules, and clear labeling of food condition.

Pickup Handoff and Notifications

Send reminders to both sides, generate pickup codes or order IDs, and support quick status updates when food is collected or canceled.

Personas

Designed for restaurants, locals, and pickup partners.

The experience balances efficiency and trust so each audience sees the details they need without extra operational burden.

Restaurant Manager

Publishes unsold food listings and manages pickup fulfillment without adding operational burden or risking customer complaints.

Value-Seeking Local Customer

Browses nearby discounted food and reserves pickups with confidence that the food is fresh, safe, and available when promised.

Pickup Partner

Collects and delivers reserved orders with clear pickup timing, accurate order details, and low-friction handoff.
