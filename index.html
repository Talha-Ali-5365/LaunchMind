Features

A signal-driven outbound workflow for SDRs, RevOps, and sales leaders.

Every section mirrors the product spec: surface the right account, qualify it, draft outreach, enforce approvals, and keep CRM state accurate when replies land.

Configurable Signal-Based Lead Discovery

Detect leads that match ICP rules using job changes, funding events, hiring spikes, tech-stack changes, website engagement, intent data, and account activity.

Warm Lead Qualification Rules

Define warmth with filters for account fit, trigger recency, persona match, and disqualifiers so only relevant leads enter outreach.

AI Personalized Outreach Drafts

Generate tailored email and sequence copy using lead signals, account context, and approved tone of voice for sales and revops buyers.

Human Approval and Compliance Guardrails

Require review before send, enforce opt-out suppression, track consent and unsubscribe status, and block non-compliant sends with audit visibility.

Smart Sequencing, CRM Handoff, and Packaging

Run follow-up sequences until reply, stop automatically, and hand off status, notes, and engagement history to CRM with a modern, trustworthy brand experience.

Pricing That Fits the Team

Positioned for per-seat or active-sequence packaging so the offer feels practical for teams balancing throughput, compliance, and forecastable spend.
