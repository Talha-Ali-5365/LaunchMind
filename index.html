Features

Built to automate the work reps hate without removing the controls RevOps needs.

Configurable Signal Ingestion

Ingest signals from funding, hiring, website activity, intent, and CRM events, then map them to ICP rules to surface relevant accounts and contacts.

ICP Lead Discovery and Prioritization

Score and rank leads based on configurable ICP criteria so reps see the highest-value prospects first instead of manually prospecting.

AI Personalized Outreach Drafts

Generate warm emails and sequence steps using lead context, trigger signals, and company research, with editable drafts for rep review.

Human-in-the-Loop Approval and Smart Follow-Ups

Require approval before sending, then run follow-up sequences that adapt to replies, bounces, opt-outs, and CRM handoff conditions.

Compliance and Billing Controls

Enforce opt-out handling, suppression lists, audit logs, and usage tracking to support per-seat or active-sequence pricing models.

Who it serves

Designed for reps, RevOps, and sales leaders who need motion and control in the same workflow.

WarmTrail reflects the reality of modern outbound: the best signals are mixed, the best sequences need review, and the best outcomes only matter when they land in CRM without extra admin work.

Outbound Sales Rep

Finds the right prospects faster because leads are automatically surfaced from relevant signals, ranked by ICP fit, and packaged with editable outreach drafts.

Outcome: less manual prospecting, more reply-worthy outreach.

RevOps Manager

Keeps automation aligned to policy with required approval, opt-out handling, suppression lists, audit logs, and usage tracking.

Outcome: compliant sequences that still move quickly.

Sales leader visibility

Pipeline visibility without manual reporting.

See which signals and sequence paths create replies, then hand qualified conversations to CRM automatically so opportunities progress without extra admin work.
