Features

Designed for the last hour of service.

Every part of the product reflects closing-time realities: quick listing, local discovery, dependable checkout, and trust controls that set clear expectations before anyone arrives.

Restaurant Listing Flow

Create end-of-day offers in a few taps by selecting food type, quantity, pickup window, discount, and availability status.

Nearby Discovery and Search

Show a map and list of nearby discounted food offers with filters for cuisine, price, pickup time, and dietary needs.

Reservation and Checkout

Reserve or purchase instantly, lock inventory, and send confirmation with pickup instructions to reduce missed handoffs.

Trust and Safety Controls

Require freshness notes, allergen disclosure, pickup cutoff times, cancellation rules, and food condition labels.

Pickup Handoff and Notifications

Send reminders, generate pickup codes or order IDs, and support quick status updates when food is collected or canceled.

Personas

Built for busy kitchens, local deal hunters, and efficient handoffs.

The experience maps to the real needs in the spec: restaurant managers need low-friction publishing, customers need confidence and speed, and pickup partners need unambiguous handoff details.

Restaurant Manager

Reduce waste without adding burden.

Publish remaining food with quantity, pickup window, and discount so closing staff can recover revenue before the night ends.

Local Customer

Find affordable meals with confidence.

Browse nearby deals quickly while seeing freshness, allergen, timing, and cancellation details up front.

Pickup Partner

Keep handoffs clear and on time.

Use order IDs, notifications, and pickup codes to collect food without confusion or disputes.

Workflow clarity

Every order has a cutoff, a note, and a handoff path.

That keeps the marketplace trustworthy, especially when a customer is reserving food near closing time.
