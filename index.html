Features

Designed around how students and parents actually choose a tutor.

From discovery to payment, every interaction is optimized for speed, confidence, and local marketplace quality.

Search and filter with precision

Students can narrow tutors by subject, grade, location, availability, and price to quickly find relevant matches.

Tutor profile pages that build confidence

Each profile shows bio, subjects, grade levels, rates, experience, reviews, and credentials for straightforward comparison.

Messaging, booking, and payments in one flow

In-app messages, calendar confirmation, secure checkout, receipts, and cancellation rules keep sessions organized.

Trust signals for safer decisions

Verification badges, identity checks, review history, and rating signals help parents book with greater peace of mind.

One-city launch controls

Curated onboarding, manual review, and basic safety support keep the marketplace focused on liquidity and quality in a single region.

MVP launch outcome

A trustworthy tutoring marketplace that works for students, tutors, and parents from day one.
