Features

A signal-driven outbound workflow for SDRs, RevOps, and sales leaders.

Every section reflects the spec: surface the right account, qualify it, draft outreach, enforce approvals, and keep CRM state accurate when replies land.

Configurable Signal-Based Lead Discovery

Detect leads that match ICP rules using job changes, funding events, hiring spikes, tech-stack changes, website engagement, intent data, and account activity.

Warm Lead Qualification Rules

Let teams define warmth with filters for account fit, trigger recency, persona match, and disqualifiers so only relevant leads enter outreach.

AI Personalized Outreach Drafts

Generate tailored email and sequence copy using lead signals, account context, and approved tone of voice for sales and revops buyers.

Human-in-the-Loop Approval and Compliance Guardrails

Require review before send, enforce opt-out suppression, track consent and unsubscribe status, and block non-compliant sends with audit visibility.

Smart Sequencing, CRM Handoff, and Packaging

Run follow-up sequences until reply, then stop automatically and hand off status, notes, and engagement history to CRM; support per-seat or active-sequence pricing.

Audit-Friendly Reporting

Give managers and RevOps clean visibility into sends, approvals, reply stops, and downstream pipeline outcomes without extra manual tracking.

Designed for review-first outbound

The product experience stays grounded in the approval path: qualification rules, message review, compliance checks, and CRM-ready handoff.

Workflow

From signal to approved sequence to CRM handoff.

01

Detect

Match ICP rules against signals like funding, hiring spikes, tech changes, engagement, and intent.

02

Qualify

Apply recency, persona, fit, and disqualifier filters to keep only warm leads in the queue.

03

Approve

04

Handoff

Stop on reply, capture notes and engagement history, and sync the outcome cleanly into CRM.
