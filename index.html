Features

Everything families need to choose tutoring confidently

The experience follows the core user stories from the spec: quick discovery for students, safe comparison for parents, and a trust-building workflow for tutors.

Local Tutor Search

Find nearby tutors by subject, location, price, availability, and distance so Mia can get help fast and within budget.

Tutor Profiles and Comparison

Compare credentials, hourly rates, availability, intro videos, and reviews side by side so Daniel can decide with clarity.

Booking and Scheduling

Request, confirm, reschedule, and cancel sessions in-app with clear availability and calendar-friendly flows.

In-App Messaging and Payments

Coordinate safely in chat and handle fees, refunds, and cancellations through secure payments that keep everyone aligned.

Ratings, Reviews, and Trust Signals

Show verification badges, ratings, and post-session reviews so trust is visible before a booking is confirmed.

Tutor Growth Tools

Help Aisha set her profile, availability, cancellation rules, and reviews so she can attract students transparently.
