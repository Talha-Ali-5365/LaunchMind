Features

A complete outbound workflow, built around control and quality.

Each section maps directly to the product spec so reviewers can trace the story from lead discovery to compliant follow-up and pricing meter boundaries.

Signal-Based ICP Lead Discovery

Automatically finds leads matching configurable ICP criteria from intent, firmographic, technographic, hiring, funding, and engagement signals.

Human-In-The-Loop Outreach Drafting

Generates personalized warm outreach drafts for each lead and requires approval before sending to ensure quality and control.

Smart Follow-Up Sequences

Runs automated follow-up steps until a reply is received, the lead opts out, or the conversation is handed off to CRM.

Compliance and Opt-Out Controls

Provides unsubscribe handling, suppression lists, approval gates, and audit trails to support compliance-first outbound workflows.

Seat and Active-Sequence Pricing Metering

Supports pricing by user seat for team access and by active sequence for usage-based scaling, with limits tied to outreach volume and automation activity.

Workflow guarantee

Reply-aware handoff

Sequences stop when a prospect replies, opts out, or is handed off to CRM, preventing duplicate outreach and keeping records clean.

Pricing & usage

Simple metering for teams and automation volume.

WarmTrail is designed to scale predictably: one layer covers team access, and another tracks how much active automation is running at any moment.

Seat pricing

Access for sales and revops teams

Seats are best for managers, operators, and reps who need login access, approvals, reporting, and collaboration in one shared workspace.

  • Team access for discovery, drafting, approvals, and reporting
  • Good fit for Sales Managers, RevOps, and SDR pods
  • Controls for role-based review and compliance oversight

Active-sequence metering

Usage-based scaling for outbound automation

Active sequences measure the automation actually running for leads and accounts. This keeps billing aligned with outreach activity instead of simply dormant setup.

  • Track sequences that are live, sending, or awaiting response
  • Helps tie product limits to outreach volume and automation load
  • Transparent for operators planning scale and governance
