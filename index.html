Features that keep collections moving

Every feature maps to the freelancer invoice-reminder value proposition: monitor unpaid invoices, send reminders at the right time, and stop or escalate messages based on payment outcomes.

Designed around the user stories

The revised layout also addresses the key user flows in the spec: first-time setup, importing invoices, and automatically stopping reminders once payment is received.

1. Connect and import

Set up the account, connect invoice data, and import unpaid invoices so follow-up starts quickly without manual re-entry.

2. Monitor aging and schedule reminders

See unpaid invoices by age, amount, and urgency while reminders are automatically scheduled around due dates and overdue intervals.

3. Confirm payment and stop outreach

Once payment is received, reminders stop automatically; bounces, replies, and overdue escalation are handled without extra admin work.

Keep follow-up consistent, polite, and tied to real invoice status.
