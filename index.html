Features

Built for the signals, approvals, and hygiene that modern outbound demands.

Each feature maps directly to the workflow sales, RevOps, and managers need to move faster without losing control.

Configurable Signal-Based ICP Finder

Detects and scores prospects from signals like hiring, funding, tech stack changes, website activity, and account events so SDRs can prioritize the right accounts first.

Personalized Outreach Drafting

Generates tailored warm outreach using account and prospect context, while keeping drafts editable before anything is sent.

Human-in-the-Loop Approval Flow

Routes drafts and sequence changes through review with clear edit, approve, reject, and pause controls to protect quality and compliance.

Smart Follow-Up Sequencing and Handoff

Runs conditional follow-ups until reply, opt-out, or a threshold is reached, then stops and hands off qualified activity to the CRM.

Compliance and Packaging Controls

Supports opt-out handling, suppression lists, consent-aware sending rules, audit trails, and packaging options such as per-seat or active-sequence pricing.
