Features

Everything families need to choose tutoring confidently

The experience is structured around the core user stories in the product spec: fast discovery for students, safe comparison for parents, and a reputation-building workflow for tutors.

Local Tutor Search

Find nearby tutors by subject, location, price, availability, and distance so Mia can get help fast without overpaying.

Tutor Profiles and Comparison

Compare credentials, hourly rates, intro videos, availability, and reviews side by side so Daniel can choose with clarity.

Booking and Scheduling

Request, confirm, reschedule, and cancel sessions in-app with calendar-friendly flows that keep everyone aligned.

In-App Messaging and Payments

Coordinate safely in chat and handle session fees, refunds, and cancellations through a secure payment flow.

Ratings, Reviews, and Trust Signals

Surface verification badges, ratings, and post-session reviews to reassure parents and help tutors build reputation transparently.

Trustworthy by design

A clear marketplace flow helps families compare confidently while giving tutors a transparent way to earn, collect reviews, and manage cancellation rules.
