Features

Signal-Based ICP Lead Discovery

Automatically finds leads matching configurable ICP criteria from intent, firmographic, technographic, hiring, funding, and engagement signals.

Human-In-The-Loop Outreach Drafting

Generates personalized warm outreach drafts for each lead and requires approval before sending to ensure quality and control.

Smart Follow-Up Sequences

Runs automated follow-up steps until a reply is received, the lead opts out, or the conversation is handed off to CRM.

Compliance and Opt-Out Controls

Provides unsubscribe handling, suppression lists, approval gates, and audit trails to support compliance-first outbound workflows.

Seat and Active-Sequence Pricing Metering

Supports pricing by user seat for team access and by active sequence for usage-based scaling, with product limits tied to outreach volume and automation activity.

How it works

Signal in, approval out, follow-up that knows when to stop.

This page is designed around the operating model in the spec: qualify leads from signals, draft outreach with human approval, sequence follow-ups, then stop on reply or hand off to CRM.

  1. 1. Find the right leads. Configure the signal mix for your ICP and surface prospects that match the team’s outbound criteria.
  2. 2. Review every draft. WarmTrail generates personalized outreach, but approval gates keep RevOps in control before any send.
  3. 3. Sequence responsibly. Automated follow-up continues until reply, opt-out, or CRM handoff, so SDRs don’t duplicate work.
