Features

Built to automate the work reps hate without loosening RevOps controls.

Configurable Signal Ingestion

Ingest signals from multiple sources such as funding, hiring, website activity, intent, and CRM events, then map them to ICP rules to surface relevant accounts and contacts.

ICP Lead Discovery and Prioritization

Score and rank leads based on configurable ICP criteria so reps see the highest-value prospects first.

AI Personalized Outreach Drafts

Generate warm outreach emails and sequence steps using lead context, trigger signals, and company research, with editable drafts for rep review.

Human-in-the-Loop Approval and Smart Follow-Ups

Require approval before sending, then run follow-up sequences that adapt to replies, bounces, opt-outs, and CRM handoff conditions.

Compliance and Billing Controls

Enforce opt-out handling, suppression lists, audit logs, and usage tracking to support per-seat or active-sequence pricing models.
