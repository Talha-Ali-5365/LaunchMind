Features

A complete outbound workflow, built around control and quality.

Every major step maps to a feature in the spec: discovery, drafting, sequencing, compliance, and pricing metering.

Signal-Based ICP Lead Discovery

Automatically find leads matching configurable ICP criteria from intent, firmographic, technographic, hiring, funding, and engagement signals.

Human-In-The-Loop Outreach Drafting

Generate personalized warm outreach drafts for each lead, then require approval before sending so sales and RevOps keep control.

Smart Follow-Up Sequences

Run automated follow-ups until a reply is received, the lead opts out, or the conversation is handed off to CRM.

Compliance and Opt-Out Controls

Support unsubscribe handling, suppression lists, approval gates, and audit trails for compliance-first outbound workflows.

Seat and Active-Sequence Pricing Metering

Meter access by user seat and usage by active sequence, with limits tied to outreach volume and automation activity.

Why it matters

WarmTrail reduces manual prospecting, standardizes outreach quality, and prevents duplicate effort while preserving a compliant handoff to CRM.

Built for governance

Approval first: no outreach goes out without human review.

Reply-aware automation: sequences stop when prospects answer or opt out.

CRM handoff: conversations flow into the system of record without drift.

Usage visibility: seats and active sequences keep scaling predictable.
