Features

Built for the exact motions sales teams need to move faster without losing control.

Configurable Signal Ingestion

Ingest signals from funding, hiring, website activity, intent, and CRM events, then map them to ICP rules to surface the right accounts and contacts.

ICP Lead Discovery and Prioritization

Score and rank leads against configurable ICP criteria so reps start with the highest-value prospects instead of manual prospecting.

AI Personalized Outreach Drafts

Generate warm emails and sequence steps using lead context, trigger signals, and company research, with editable drafts for rep review.

Human-in-the-Loop Approval and Smart Follow-Ups

Require approval before sending, then adapt follow-up sequences to replies, bounces, opt-outs, and CRM handoff conditions.

Compliance and Billing Controls

Enforce opt-out handling, suppression lists, audit logs, and usage tracking to support per-seat or active-sequence pricing models.

How it works

A workflow that keeps reps moving and RevOps in control.

1. Signals become prioritized accounts

WarmTrail ingests buying signals, applies ICP rules, and surfaces relevant opportunities for the team.

2. Drafts stay editable and approved

AI-assisted outreach drafts are reviewed by a human before anything is sent, preserving tone and compliance.

3. Replies route back to CRM

Qualified conversations are handed off automatically so pipeline progresses without manual admin work.

RevOps confidence

Audit trails, approvals, suppression lists, and usage tracking are built into the sequence engine.

Sales momentum

Reps spend more time on conversations and less time hunting for context or sending inconsistent follow-ups.
