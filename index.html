Core marketplace features

The product is designed to make surplus food easy to list, easy to find, and easy to collect—while keeping trust, safety, and pickup verification visible at every step.

1

Restaurant Surplus Listings

Restaurants post end-of-day inventory with item descriptions, quantity, pickup windows, discounted prices, and freshness cutoffs.

2

Nearby Deal Discovery

Buyers browse discounted food sorted by distance, pickup time, price, and category to quickly find relevant offers nearby.

3

Reserve and Pay Ahead

Buyers reserve items and pay in-app to reduce uncertainty, no-shows, and friction at the counter.

4

Trust and Safety Controls

Restaurant verification, food handling guidance, allergen notes, and freshness rules help buyers make informed decisions.

5

Pickup Confirmation Flow

QR code or order code confirmation validates handoff, prevents fraud, and closes the transaction cleanly.
