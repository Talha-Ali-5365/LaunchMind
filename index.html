Features

Built for the last hour of service.

Every part of the product reflects the realities of closing time: quick listing, local discovery, reliable reservation flow, and trust controls that set clear expectations.

Restaurant Listing Flow

Create end-of-day offers in a few taps by choosing food type, quantity, pickup window, discount, and availability status.

Nearby Discovery and Search

Show a map and list of nearby discounted food offers with filters for cuisine, price, pickup time, and dietary needs.

Reservation and Checkout

Reserve items instantly, lock inventory, and send confirmation with pickup instructions to reduce missed handoffs.

Trust and Safety Controls

Require freshness notes, allergen disclosure, pickup cutoff times, cancellation rules, and food condition labels.

Pickup Handoff and Notifications

Send reminders, generate pickup codes or order IDs, and support quick status updates when food is collected or canceled.

Personas

Designed for the people keeping food in circulation.

The experience balances restaurant operations, customer confidence, and handoff simplicity so each side can move quickly without uncertainty.

Restaurant Manager

Publishes listings and manages fulfillment

Needs to reduce waste and recover revenue without adding operational burden or risking customer complaints.

Value-Seeking Local Customer

Browses nearby offers and reserves pickups

Wants affordable meals from nearby restaurants with confidence that food is fresh, safe, and available when promised.

Pickup Partner

Collects and delivers reserved orders

Needs clear pickup timing, accurate order details, and low-friction handoff to avoid delays and disputes.
