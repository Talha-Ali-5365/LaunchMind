Features

Built for the closing shift, the deal hunt, and the compliance checklist.

Every feature maps to the realities of end-of-day food: quick posting, controlled discounts, safe disclosures, reliable pickup verification, and clean refund handling.

End-of-day inventory posting

Create listings for unsold items with quantity, category, photo, allergen notes, pickup window, and expiration cutoff in a few taps.

Discount and pricing rules

Support fixed-price bundles or percentage discounts with guardrails such as minimum floors, time-based markdowns, and late-close listing limits.

Reservation, payment, and pickup flow

Customers reserve and pay in-app, receive a pickup QR/code, and restaurant staff verify the order at handoff to prevent double-selling.

Food safety and disclosure requirements

Listings must include ingredients, allergens, storage notes, prepared time, and pickup-by time before a deal can go live.

Cancellation and refund handling

The system supports restaurant-initiated cancellations for stock issues and customer cancellations within defined windows, with automated refunds and status updates.

How it works

A workflow that respects the final hour of service.

1. Post

Restaurant manager sets the listing

Unsold food is posted with price rules, quantity, expiration cutoff, and compliance disclosures so the deal is ready to publish quickly.

2. Reserve

Nearby customers purchase with confidence

Deal-seeking customers browse nearby offers, reserve and pay in-app, then receive a code tied to the pickup window.

3. Handoff

Closing staff verify and complete the sale

At pickup, staff confirm the code, reduce the risk of overselling, and mark the order fulfilled without slowing down closing tasks.
