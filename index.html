Features

Everything from signal capture to safe handoff is designed to stay controlled.

WarmTrail is built to help revenue teams act quickly without sacrificing quality, compliance, or ownership clarity.

01

Configurable ICP Signal Sourcing

Pull leads from hiring, funding, tech stack changes, intent data, and job changes, then score them against ICP rules to prioritize the best-fit opportunities first.

02

Lead Qualification and Prioritization Engine

Apply rule-based and AI-assisted qualification to rank leads, suppress poor fits, detect duplicates, and surface the best prospects before the queue gets noisy.

03

AI-Personalized Outreach Drafts with Approval Gates

Generate tailored first-touch and follow-up messages from prospect context, but require human approval before any send action.

04

Smart Follow-up Sequencing and CRM Handoff

Run adaptive follow-up sequences until a reply is received, the lead opts out, or the workflow hands off to the CRM with logged status and ownership.

05

Compliance, Trust, and Packaging Controls

Opt-out management, suppression lists, audit logs, sending guardrails, and transparent per-seat or active-sequence pricing are built into the product story for sales and revops buyers.

Pricing & packaging

Transparent packaging that matches how revenue teams buy software.

Make the model explicit so buyers know whether they want per-seat simplicity or active-sequence efficiency. Pricing clarity is part of the trust story.

Per-seat package

Best for team adoption

Designed for sales teams that want predictable access per rep. Includes approved drafting, signal sourcing, sequencing, and compliance controls in one governed workspace.

  • Clear seat-based billing for reps and managers
  • Shared governance: approval gates, audit logs, suppression lists
  • CRM handoff and ownership logging included

Active-sequence package

Best for efficiency

Built for teams that want to pay based on the active workflows they run. Ideal when outreach volume changes across campaigns and routing needs to stay flexible.

  • Transparent active-sequence pricing with no hidden send model
  • Guardrails for opt-outs, compliance, and send approvals
  • Easy fit for small B2B teams without process debt
