Features

Built around how families choose a tutor and how tutors grow a local client base.

The product balances speed, trust, and market quality from discovery through payment.

Student tutor search and filtering

Search by subject, grade, location, availability, and price to narrow quickly to relevant tutors.

Tutor profile pages

Profiles show bio, subjects, grade levels, rates, experience, reviews, and credentials for side-by-side comparison.

Booking, messaging, and payment flow

In-app messaging, calendar confirmation, secure payment, receipts, and cancellation rules keep sessions organized.

Tutor verification and trust signals

Identity checks, credential verification, ratings, badges, and review history help parents book with confidence.

MVP launch controls for one city

Curated onboarding, manual review, and basic safety support keep supply quality high in a single city or region.

Outcome

A trustworthy tutoring marketplace that feels fast for students, safe for parents, and practical for tutors.
