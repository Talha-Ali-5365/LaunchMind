Features

Built around how families choose a tutor and how tutors win local demand.

The product balances speed, trust, and marketplace quality from discovery through payment.

Student tutor search and filtering

Search by subject, grade, location, availability, and price to quickly narrow to relevant tutors.

Tutor profile pages

Profiles show bio, subjects, grade levels, rates, experience, reviews, and credentials for side-by-side comparison.

Booking, messaging, and payment flow

In-app messaging, calendar confirmation, secure payment, receipts, and cancellation rules keep sessions organized.

Tutor verification and trust signals

Identity checks, credential verification, ratings, badges, and review history help parents book with confidence.

MVP launch controls for one city

Curated onboarding, manual review, and basic safety support keep supply quality high in a single city or region.

Outcome

A trustworthy tutoring marketplace that feels fast for students, safe for parents, and practical for tutors.

The marketplace turns discovery into a simple sequence: search, compare, verify, chat, book, and pay.

Subject

Get more local tutoring bookings with LaunchMind

Hi Jordan,

We’re launching LaunchMind, a mobile marketplace built to help local tutors get discovered by students and parents who are actively looking for support. Instead of spending time on outreach, scheduling back-and-forth, and follow-ups, you can create a verified profile, set your subjects and rates, and receive bookings in one place.

Parents and students can filter by subject, grade, location, availability, and price, then compare tutors using reviews, credentials, and trust signals. When they’re ready, they can message, book, and pay securely in the app.

We’re starting with a one-city launch so the marketplace stays curated, safe, and high quality. If you’re interested in being part of the first tutor cohort, I’d love to share more details.

Best,
The LaunchMind team
