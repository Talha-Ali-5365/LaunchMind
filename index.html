Features

Built for the closing shift, the customer deal hunt, and the compliance checklist.

Every feature maps to the realities of end-of-day food: quick posting, controlled discounts, safe disclosures, and reliable pickup verification.

End-of-day inventory posting

Create listings for unsold items with quantity, category, photo, allergen notes, pickup window, and expiration cutoff in a few taps.

Discount and pricing rules

Support fixed-price bundles or percentage discounts with guardrails such as minimum floors, time-based markdowns, and late-close listing limits.

Reservation, payment, and pickup flow

Customers reserve and pay in-app, receive a pickup QR/code, and staff verify the order at handoff to prevent double-selling.

Food safety and disclosure requirements

Listings include ingredients, allergens, storage notes, prepared time, and pickup-by time before a deal can go live.

Cancellation and refund handling

Restaurant-initiated cancellations and customer cancellations within defined windows trigger automated refunds and live status updates.

How it works

A low-friction flow that fits the final hour of service.

The platform is designed around the real constraints of closing time: minimal data entry, controlled discounts, clear pickup windows, and instant verification at the door.
