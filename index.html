Features

The full workflow, from signal to approved send to CRM handoff.

Every feature maps directly to the product spec: signal discovery, qualification rules, AI drafting, compliance controls, and sequencing that stops on reply.

Configurable Signal-Based Lead Discovery

Detect leads that match ICP rules using configurable signals such as job changes, funding events, hiring spikes, tech-stack changes, website engagement, intent data, and account activity.

Warm Lead Qualification Rules

Let teams define what counts as a warm lead with filters for account fit, trigger recency, persona match, and disqualifiers so only relevant leads enter outreach.

AI Personalized Outreach Drafts

Generate tailored email and sequence copy using lead signals, account context, and approved tone of voice for sales and revops buyers.

Human-in-the-Loop Approval and Compliance Guardrails

Require review before send, enforce opt-out suppression, track consent and unsubscribe status, and block non-compliant sends with audit visibility.

Smart Sequencing, CRM Handoff, and Packaging

Run follow-up sequences until reply, then stop automatically and hand off status, notes, and engagement history to CRM; support pricing by per-seat or active-sequence with a modern, trustworthy brand experience.

How it works

A simple workflow that keeps ownership and compliance clear.

1. Discover

Signals surface the accounts most likely to respond: job changes, funding events, hiring spikes, tech-stack changes, website engagement, intent data, and account activity.

2. Approve

Human review protects deliverability and compliance with opt-out suppression, consent tracking, unsubscribe blocking, and audit visibility.

3. Sync

Sequences stop on reply and hand notes, status, and engagement history back to CRM so pipeline reporting stays accurate.
