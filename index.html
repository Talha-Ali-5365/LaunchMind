Features

Built for the workflow behind trusted outbound.

Every feature maps directly to the operating needs in the spec: better signals, safer copy, stronger approval control, sequence automation, and compliance guardrails.

01

Signal-Based ICP Lead Finder

Automatically discover leads from hiring, funding, technology changes, website activity, and intent criteria with saved signal rules designed for an MVP that can expand into richer enrichment later.

02

AI Personalized Outreach Drafts

Generate warm first-touch emails and follow-up copy tailored to each lead and signal context, with editable templates and guardrails for tone, facts, and compliance.

03

Human Approval Workflow

Require review before sending with approve, edit, and reject actions, while logging who approved what for a clean trail of accountability.

04

Smart Sequence Engine

Run multi-step follow-up sequences until a reply, opt-out, or CRM handoff, while tracking states like drafted, pending approval, queued, sent, replied, paused, opted out, and handed off.

05

Compliance and Packaging Controls

Enforce unsubscribe handling, suppression lists, and sending rules with admin visibility for per-seat or per-active-sequence packaging assumptions and usage tracking.

How it works

A safer outbound loop from signal to handoff.

1

Find the right accounts

WarmTrail identifies leads that match the configured ICP signals so SDRs can focus on the highest-potential accounts.

2

Approve polished outreach

Sales managers review AI-drafted emails before they send, preserving accuracy, brand voice, and compliance.

3

Keep sequences synchronized

RevOps can stop sequences on reply, opt-out, or CRM handoff so outreach remains compliant and the pipeline stays clean.
