Core product features

The experience is designed around the full end-of-day workflow: posting surplus, finding the best nearby offer, paying with less friction, and confirming pickup cleanly.

1

Restaurant Surplus Listings

Restaurants post item descriptions, quantity, pickup windows, discount pricing, and freshness cutoffs so end-of-day inventory is easy to sell.

2

Nearby Deal Discovery

Buyers browse discounted meals sorted by distance, pickup time, price, and category to quickly find relevant offers nearby.

3

Reserve and Pay Ahead

Reserve items and pay in-app to reduce uncertainty, cut no-shows, and make pickup faster for both buyers and restaurants.

4

Trust and Safety Controls

Verification badges, food handling guidance, allergen notes, and freshness rules build confidence and support informed decisions.

5

Pickup Confirmation Flow

QR code or order code confirmation validates handoff, prevents fraud, and closes the transaction cleanly at pickup.
