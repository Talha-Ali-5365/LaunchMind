Built for surplus food, nearby discovery, and clean pickup handoff.

The platform is organized around the operational flow restaurants need and the decision flow buyers want: post the surplus, surface the best nearby deal, reserve and pay, then confirm pickup with confidence.

1

Restaurant Surplus Listings

Post end-of-day inventory with item details, quantity, pickup window, discount price, and freshness cutoff so selling surplus stays simple.

2

Nearby Deal Discovery

Surface discounted meals by distance, pickup time, price, and category to help buyers quickly find relevant offers close to them.

3

Reserve and Pay Ahead

Let buyers secure items and pay in-app, reducing uncertainty for customers and cutting down on no-shows for restaurants.

4

Trust and Safety Controls

Show restaurant verification, food handling guidance, allergy and allergen notes, and freshness rules to build confidence before purchase.

5

Pickup Confirmation Flow

Support QR or order code confirmation at pickup to verify handoff, prevent fraud, and close the transaction cleanly.
