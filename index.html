Features

Built for posting, pricing, pickup, and the safety checks that keep the deal live.

LaunchMind maps directly to the end-of-day workflow: fast posting, pricing guardrails, verified pickup handoff, compliance disclosures, and refund handling when plans change.

End-of-Day Inventory Posting

Create listings for unsold items with quantity, category, photo, allergen notes, pickup window, and expiration cutoff in a few taps.

Discount and Pricing Rules

Support fixed-price bundles or percentage discounts with guardrails such as minimum price floors, time-based markdowns, and late-close listing limits.

Reservation, Payment, and Pickup Flow

Customers reserve and pay in-app, receive a pickup QR/code, and restaurant staff verify the order at handoff to prevent double-selling.

Food Safety and Disclosure Requirements

Require ingredients, allergens, storage notes, prepared time, and pickup-by time before a deal can go live.

Cancellation and Refund Handling

Support restaurant-initiated cancellations for stock issues and customer cancellations within defined windows, with automated refunds and status updates.

How it works

A simple flow for the last hour of service.

01

Manager posts remaining inventory

They set quantity, price rule, pickup window, and required disclosures so the listing is ready before close.

02

Closing staff confirms handoff

As orders are reserved, staff update quantities, prevent overselling, and scan a code at pickup.

03

Customer reserves and picks up nearby

Deal-seeking customers browse nearby offers, pay in app, and pick up confidently before the cutoff time.
